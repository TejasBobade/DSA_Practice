3. How to approach a new problems?
    - 1. Read the problem statement twice
    - 2. Analyze Input/Output examples
    - 3. Visualize with pen and paper
    - 4. Start with a brute force approach
    - 5. Optimize youe solution
    - Leverage Unused Information
        ex. - binary search
            - two pointer approach
    - Precompute Information
        ex. - prefix sum
            - frequency counts
            - Hash Tables
    -  Make Time VS Space TradeOFF
    -  Do a data structure BrainStorm
    6. Analyze time and space complexity

4. How much time should i spend on each problem?
at starting even easy problems may take time
your main goal in beginning should be learning and understanding the problem deeply
- 30 - 60 minutes for each problem if unable to solve read solution and see top solution in foram and don't just move on grasp why this solution works and how to apply it to similar problem. 
If you thing you want revist problem them come back and solve it again
- Avoid Memorization    
    1. You'll quickly forget memorized solutions
    2. Your problem solving skills won't improve
    3. You will struggle in interviews
    - focus on undertanding the solution even if it takes hours break it down step by stepand ask yourself
    - Why Does this approach works?
    - what's the key insight that simplifies the problem
- Practice in a timed environment
    - Easy - 10-15 Minutes
    - Medium - 30 minutes
    - hard - 60 minutes
    - Participate in leetcode virtual contest
    - regularly revisit concepts and problem you already solved specially the ones you found challenging
